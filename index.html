Common Questions

FAQ Answered

Everything you need to know before walking through our doors for the first time.

01Do I need any experience to join?

Not at all. We welcome complete beginners and experienced lifters alike. Our trainers are here to guide you from day one if needed.

02How do I sign up for a membership?

Fill the online registration form and we’ll confirm your membership via WhatsApp within a few hours. You can also call us on 0300 4638281 or walk in during open hours.

03Can I switch my membership plan later?

Yes. If your goals change or you want to upgrade, just contact us and we'll adjust your plan from the next billing cycle with no penalty.

04What are the gym timings?

We run 3 sessions daily, 6 days a week (closed Sunday). Morning: 6:30 AM – 9:00 AM. Ladies only: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM. Evening: 5:00 PM – 12:00 AM (midnight).

05Is the Fat Burning Package worth it?

Absolutely — it's our most complete package. You get a personal trainer, custom diet plan, unlimited cardio access, and weekly check-ins, all designed around fat loss specifically.

06Do you offer trial sessions?

Yes. We encourage you to visit us first. Come during any open session, have a look around, and talk to one of our trainers before committing to any plan.
